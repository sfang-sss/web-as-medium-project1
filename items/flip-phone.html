Flip Phone

Timeline

  1. 1996 — Motorola released the StarTAC, the first clamshell mobile phone.

  2. 2004 — The ultra-thin Motorola Razr V3 became a fashion icon and one of the best-selling phones ever.

  3. 2007 — The iPhone arrived, and touchscreens quickly pushed flip phones aside.

  4. 2020s — “Dumbphones” returned as young people looked for ways to spend less time on their screens.

The flip phone folded in half to protect its keypad and screen, and snapping it shut became the satisfying way to end a call. In the 2000s, phones were decorated with charms and stickers, and texting meant pressing the same key several times for one letter.

Smartphones replaced it, but the flip phone never fully disappeared. Its folding form returned in the foldable phone, and its limits — no feed, no apps, just calls and texts — are now part of its appeal. Like the corded telephone before it, it is remembered as a device you put down when the conversation was over.
