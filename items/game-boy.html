Game Boy

Timeline

  1. 1989 — Nintendo released the Game Boy, bundled with Tetris in North America.

  2. 1998 — The Game Boy Color added a color screen.

  3. 2001 — The Game Boy Advance brought near-console graphics to a handheld.

  4. 2004 — Sony's PSP and the Nintendo DS turned handhelds into multimedia devices.

The Game Boy was a handheld game console with a green-tinted monochrome screen, a cross-shaped D-pad, and swappable cartridges. Its battery life and durability made it the travel companion of a generation. Later, the glossy PSP added movies and music to the same pocket.

Phone games replaced most handhelds, but the chunky buttons and pixel graphics remain shorthand for play. Together with the Tamagotchi, it belongs to the era of single-purpose devices, and its low-resolution look connects to the early digital interfaces that many people now find comforting.
