Corded Telephone

Timeline

  1. 1876 — Alexander Graham Bell was granted a patent for the telephone.

  2. 1963 — The Bell System introduced Touch-Tone push-button dialing.

  3. 1980s — Cordless phones let people walk around the house during a call.

  4. 2017 — More than half of US households had given up their landline for mobile phones only.

The corded telephone sat in one place — on a desk, a kitchen wall, a bedside table — connected to the landline network. The coiled cord limited how far you could walk, and everyone in the house could hear who you were talking to.

Mobile phones made it portable and then made it unnecessary. The red push-button phone has since become an object of decoration and nostalgia, a reminder of a time when a call happened in a specific place. Its descendants are the flip phone and the foldable phone; its ringing, unknown-caller suspense survives only in memory.
