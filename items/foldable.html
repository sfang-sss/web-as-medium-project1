Foldable Phone

Timeline

  1. 2018 — Royole announced the FlexPai, the first commercial foldable smartphone.

  2. 2019 — Samsung released the Galaxy Fold.

  3. 2020 — Motorola brought back the Razr as a foldable phone.

  4. 2023 — Google released the Pixel Fold as more brands entered the category.

A foldable phone uses a flexible display that bends along a hinge, so the phone can open into a small tablet or close into a pocket-sized square.

It is a present-day device built on a past gesture. The snap of the flip phone returns in a premium form, showing that the future of technology sometimes moves forward by folding back on itself. It sits on the 2026 side of this collection, next to the AirPods Max.
