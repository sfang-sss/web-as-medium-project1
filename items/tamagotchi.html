Tamagotchi

Timeline

  1. 1996 — Bandai released the Tamagotchi in Japan, created by Aki Maita and Akihiro Yokoi.

  2. 1997 — It launched worldwide and was soon banned in many schools for distracting students.

  3. 2004 — Tamagotchi Connection let pets visit each other through an infrared link.

  4. 2023 — Tamagotchi Uni connected the pets to Wi-Fi and an online world.

The Tamagotchi is a digital pet that lives inside an egg-shaped keychain. Owners had to feed it, clean up after it, and play with it at all hours, or it would get sick and die.

It was one of the first devices to demand constant attention through beeps and notifications, years before the smartphone. Its pixel screen and three buttons sit alongside the Game Boy and the flip phone as the small screens of a 2000s childhood, and its caring, low-stakes routine is a big part of why adults are buying it again.
