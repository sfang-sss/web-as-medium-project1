AirPods Max

Timeline

  1. 1958 — John Koss introduced the first stereo headphones for listening to music.

  2. 2016 — Apple launched AirPods as the iPhone lost its headphone jack.

  3. 2020 — Apple announced AirPods Max, its first over-ear headphones.

  4. 2024 — AirPods Max were updated with USB-C and new colors.

AirPods Max are wireless over-ear headphones with noise cancellation and an aluminum frame. Worn on the street, they work as much as a fashion accessory as a way to listen.

They are the 2026 counterpart to wired earphones: seamless, expensive, and always connected. Placing them next to the iPod and the CD player shows how personal listening has changed — and why some people now choose the tangled cable instead.
