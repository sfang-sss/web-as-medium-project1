Wired Earphones

Timeline

  1. 1910 — Nathaniel Baldwin built headphones at his kitchen table and sold them to the US Navy.

  2. 1979 — Sony's Walkman came with lightweight headphones, making personal listening portable.

  3. 2001 — The iPod's white earbuds became a visual symbol of digital music.

  4. 2016 — The iPhone 7 removed the headphone jack, and wireless AirPods launched the same year.

Wired earphones connect to a device through a cable and a 3.5 mm jack). For two decades the white cable running from a pocket to the ears was how people showed they were listening to an iPod.

When phones dropped the headphone jack, wired earphones seemed finished. Recently they have reappeared as a style statement: the visible, slightly tangled cable reads as unpretentious and analog compared with the AirPods Max. They never need charging, and they make listening feel like something you are physically plugged into.
