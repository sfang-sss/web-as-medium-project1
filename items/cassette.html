Cassette Tape

Timeline

  1. 1963 — Philips introduced the Compact Cassette at the Berlin Radio Show.

  2. 1979 — The Sony Walkman made cassettes the soundtrack of walking, commuting, and jogging.

  3. 1983 — Cassettes outsold vinyl records in the US for the first time.

  4. 2013 — Cassette Store Day began, as labels and bands released music on tape again.

A cassette tape holds two small reels of magnetic tape inside a plastic shell. It could record as well as play, which made it the medium of mixtapes: carefully ordered songs recorded from the radio or other tapes for a friend.

The CD player replaced it with cleaner sound, and the iPod made the mixtape a playlist. Cassettes are now valued for exactly what made them inconvenient — hiss, rewinding with a pencil, and the effort that turned a tape into a personal gift. They share their revival with vinyl records.
