Liminal Spaces

Timeline

  1. 2019 — A photo of an empty yellow office was posted online with a story about “the Backrooms.”

  2. 2020 — During pandemic lockdowns, images of empty malls, hallways, and pools spread as “liminal spaces.”

  3. 2022 — Kane Parsons's Backrooms videos, styled like old camcorder footage, went viral.

  4. 2020s — Dreamcore and poolcore turned tiled pools and school computer labs into online aesthetics.

A liminal space is a place of transition — a hallway, a waiting room, an empty pool — photographed when no one is there. Online, these images feel both familiar and unsettling, as if they come from a memory you are not sure is yours.

The aesthetic leans on the look of the technology in this collection: the blur of a DV camcorder, the flash of a CCD camera, and the computer labs of early digital interfaces. It shows that nostalgia is not only about objects but also about how those objects made the past look.

Information on this page is adapted from Wikipedia: Liminal space (aesthetic), The Backrooms.
