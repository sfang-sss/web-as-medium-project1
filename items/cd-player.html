CD Player

Timeline

  1. 1982 — Sony and Philips's compact disc and the first CD players went on sale in Japan.

  2. 1984 — Sony's Discman D-50 made CDs portable.

  3. 2000 — CD sales reached their peak in the US.

  4. 2021 — CD sales in the US rose for the first time since 2004.

The compact disc stored music digitally on a disc read by a laser, offering clean sound with no hiss. Boomboxes and portable Discmans brought CDs into bedrooms, cars, and school buses, often with a binder of burned discs.

Downloads and streaming made the CD feel obsolete. Its return follows the same path as the cassette tape and vinyl: people collect albums as objects, and the skip-prone silver boombox has become part of the 2000s aesthetic, alongside the iPod that replaced it.
