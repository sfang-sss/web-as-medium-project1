Cybertruck

Timeline

  1. 1982 — Blade Runner imagined a retro-futuristic city that Tesla later cited as an inspiration.

  2. 1994 — The PlayStation popularized low-polygon 3D graphics, with the same faceted look.

  3. 2019 — Tesla unveiled the Cybertruck; its “armored” window cracked on stage.

  4. 2023 — The first Cybertrucks were delivered to customers.

The Cybertruck is an electric pickup with flat stainless-steel panels and sharp angles, designed to look like nothing else on the road.

Its shape looks less like a car from the future and more like the future as imagined in the past: low-polygon 3D models, 1980s science fiction, and the optimistic digital look of early interfaces. It belongs to the 2026 side of the collection, showing that even new objects borrow from old visions of tomorrow — much like the dreamlike images of liminal spaces.
